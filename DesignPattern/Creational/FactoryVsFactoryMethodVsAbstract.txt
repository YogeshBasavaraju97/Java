4. Factory vs Factory Method vs Abstract Factory
These names often confuse people.
Simple Factory
One class decides which object to create.
PaymentFactory
      |
      ├── UPI
      ├── Card
      └── PayPal

Factory Method
Object creation is delegated to subclasses.
Creator
   |
   ├── UPI Creator
   ├── Card Creator
   └── PayPal Creator

Abstract Factory
Creates families of related objects.
For example:
WindowsFactory
   ├── WindowsButton
   └── WindowsCheckbox

MacFactory
   ├── MacButton
   └── MacCheckbox
